The details

Membership terms.

Payment

Payment is by bank transfer or cash, arranged directly with Marina on WhatsApp. The full membership is settled before your first session.

Validity

Each membership is valid for up to 12 weeks. Emerald is 10 sessions at 1 per week; Diamond is 20 sessions at 2 per week.

Booking

After you join, message Marina on WhatsApp to reserve your sessions. Priority booking is included for members, so your preferred times are held first.

Freeze

You can freeze your membership for up to 2 weeks. The freeze does not add extra sessions, it only extends your window to complete the 10 or 20 sessions.

Sharing

Emerald is for one person. Diamond can be shared by up to 2 people on the same plan.

Availability

Memberships are limited and subject to availability. Marina holds a small number of member places at a time.
